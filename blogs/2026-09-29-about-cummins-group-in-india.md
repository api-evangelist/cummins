About Cummins Group in India vivek.more@cum… Tue, 09/29/2026 - 05:51 Established in 1962 and headquartered in Pune, Cummins Group in India is a leading provider of integrated power solutions for the industrial and automotive sectors. The company operates through 12 legal entities. With a wide-ranging portfolio, the company is engaged in designing, manufacturing, distributing, and servicing engines and powertrain-related components for commercial vehicles, gensets, and industrial markets.
